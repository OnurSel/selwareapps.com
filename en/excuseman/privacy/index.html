Privacy policy

ExcuseMan: Not My Fault

Effective date: November 24, 2026

In short

ExcuseMan is a game developed by Selware. The game does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your real name, email or phone number, and contains no analytics or ad-measurement tools of its own. The game can be played offline.

Only two kinds of information leave your device for us: (1) when you play Overtime with an internet connection, your Overtime results for the online World Leaderboard, together with the player name and country you choose (if you choose one); (2) anonymous crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) so we can fix bugs. Both are described below.

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google collects some information from your device in order to show them (see below). Without an internet connection no ads load, and the game is still fully playable.

When do ads appear?

  • Never during play. There are no ads in the middle of a level, when you lose a heart, or during dialogue. There are no banner ads.
  • Interstitial ads can appear only on the level-complete screen and on the end screen of Overtime mode. They never appear in the first 10 minutes after you open the game; after that, at most once every three levels (in Overtime, at most once every two runs).
  • Rewarded ads are entirely optional and appear only in these offers: "continue from the last checkpoint with full hearts", "double this level's earnings", "continue" in Overtime (once per run), "+50 balloons" in the costume closet (a few times a day), and "double today's reward" on the daily Time Card. You never have to watch an ad to progress.

The following stays only in your phone's own storage, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the game:

Leaderboard and achievements (Google Play Games / Game Center)

The game can work with Google Play Games on Android and Game Center on iPhone. If you are signed in to these services, the game sends the achievements you earn and your Overtime survival time to them, and you appear on the leaderboard with your player name. Google or Apple processes this information under your own account and their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store it. If you do not want to use these services, you can play the full game without signing in.

World Leaderboard (our own server)

Overtime mode has an online World Leaderboard run by us. When you play Overtime with an internet connection, the game creates an anonymous player record on our server (a random identifier stored on your device) and sends the results of your runs (survival time, shift, wave and score). If you type a player name and pick a country, those are shown next to your score; otherwise you appear as "Player". This is used only to show the public leaderboard and to filter out cheated scores. Players can report offensive names, and we review those reports. Please do not use your real name or anything offensive as your player name; offensive names are filtered. Like any web server, it also sees your IP address while it answers the request; we do not use it to identify you. To have your leaderboard entry deleted, email us with your player name.

The server runs on Render (hosting) with a Neon database (storage), which process the data on our behalf.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, the game uses Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC). When the game crashes or hits an error, it sends a crash report: the technical error details, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation identifier that is not linked to you. Crash reports do not contain your name, contacts, location or game save. Google processes this data under the Firebase terms:

https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Permissions

The game does not ask for camera, location, contacts, microphone, or file access. On Android, the ad library adds the internet and advertising ID permissions, used to load ads. On iPhone, your advertising identifier is used only if you allow it in the iOS permission prompt; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized.

European Union and United Kingdom

In these regions the game shows Google's consent form on first launch, and you choose whether to consent to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options.

Ads and third parties

Google AdMob (Google LLC) serves the ads. To show ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, it may collect your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions from your device. Google processes this data under its own policies:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. Ads shown are limited to content suitable for general and teen audiences.

The game contains no analytics or social-network libraries. Apart from the ad data and crash reports that go to Google, the World Leaderboard data described above, and the scores and achievements that go to Play Games / Game Center if you are signed in, none of your data reaches us or anyone else.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The game itself asks for no personal data from any user, including children; the leaderboard record, crash reports and the ad data that goes to Google are described above.

Deleting your data

All of the game's data on your device is deleted when you delete the game. You can also erase your progress with Settings → Reset progress. The only data we hold is your World Leaderboard record; email us with your player name to have it deleted. For data in your Google or Apple accounts, use those companies' own tools.

Changes
