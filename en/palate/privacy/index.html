Privacy policy

Palate

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Palate is a social food app that turns world cuisine into a collection. This notice explains what data we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

1. What we collect

Account data

  • Your email address — required to sign in.
  • Your username — public, shown on your profile.
  • Your password — we never store it in readable form; our identity provider (Supabase) keeps a one-way hash that we cannot read.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us.

What you choose to enter (all optional)

  • Display name, bio, profile photo, city and country.
  • Your real name. You choose who can see it; the default is "Only me".
  • Dishes you tried, your ratings, notes, photos, when and in which city you ate them, and who you were with.
  • Who you follow, your likes, comments, messages and lists.

Collected automatically

  • Technical logs created when you connect (IP address, request time), kept by our infrastructure provider for security and debugging.

What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for location permission; city is only what you type.
  • We use no advertising identifier and show no ads.
  • We do not access your contacts, your whole gallery, or your call logs.
  • The microphone opens only when you press the voice search button, and recognition runs on your device — no audio is sent to us.

2. Why we collect it

  • To create your account and identify you.
  • To build your taste passport, collection and statistics.
  • To show you posts from people you follow.
  • To detect and stop abuse (spam, harassment, fake accounts).
  • To find and fix bugs.

We do not use your data for advertising and WE DO NOT SELL IT.

3. What is visible

  • Your username, display name, profile photo and taste level are public.
  • If your account is public, your posts (the dish, rating, note, photo, city and date), the people you follow, your followers, your favourites, your favourite cuisines and your "My List" can be seen by anyone, including guests who are not signed in.
  • If you choose "Only my followers", all of these are visible only to your followers. Your follower and following COUNTS stay visible to everyone, but not who they are.
  • You choose who sees your posts: "Everyone" or "Only my followers". This choice is enforced on the server.
  • Your real name has its own setting, and it defaults to "Only me".
  • Your messages can be read only by you and the other person.
  • If you block someone, they cannot see your posts or write to you.

4. Who we share it with

We store your data with the providers needed to run the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and file storage.
  • Cloudflare — image delivery.
  • Google Play — app distribution and Google sign-in.

Otherwise we share data only upon a lawful, properly issued request from a competent public authority.

5. How long we keep it

  • We keep your data while your account is open.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, posts, photos, messages and follow relationships are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.
  • Copies may remain in technical backups for a short period; they disappear when the backup cycle completes.
  • For accounts closed due to rule violations, we keep an irreversible hash of the email address — not the address itself — to prevent re-registration.

6. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Settings → Delete my account. If you cannot reach the app, email selwareapps@gmail.com.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR and Turkish data protection law (KVKK) you may access your data, ask for correction or deletion, object to processing, and receive your data in a portable form. To exercise these: selwareapps@gmail.com We respond within 30 days at the latest.

8. Children

Palate is not directed at children under 13. We close accounts we learn belong to someone under 13.

9. Security

Access to data is restricted at the database level with row-level security rules: content you mark as private cannot be read by others even if the app interface is bypassed. Connections are encrypted. Still, we say honestly that no system is 100% secure.

10. Changes

If we update this notice we will tell you in the app. For a significant change we will ask for your consent again.

Contact: selwareapps@gmail.com

Data Protection Notice (KVKK)

Prepared by Onur Sel (Selware) as data controller under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"). This notice mirrors the Turkish original; in case of conflict the Turkish text prevails.

Data controller

Onur Sel (Selware) — selwareapps@gmail.com

Categories of personal data

  • Identity: username, display name, real name (if you enter one).
  • Contact: email address.
  • Transaction security: sign-in records, IP address, session data.
  • User activity: dishes you tried, ratings, notes, photos, comments, messages, follow relationships, city.

Purposes

  • Creating your membership and verifying your identity.
  • Delivering the services the app offers.
  • Keeping the service secure and preventing abuse.
  • Meeting legal obligations.

Legal grounds (KVKK art. 5)

  • Necessary for entering into and performing a contract — for the account and core services.
  • Legitimate interest of the controller — security and abuse detection.
  • Compliance with a legal obligation — requests from competent authorities.
  • Your explicit consent — for optional choices you make, such as showing your real name to others. You may withdraw consent at any time by changing the setting.

Method of collection

Data is collected electronically and directly from you as you use the app.

Transfers

Data is processed on Supabase (database, authentication, file storage) and Cloudflare (content delivery). These providers' servers may be located abroad; this transfer is necessary to provide the service. We make no other transfers to third parties.

Exercising your rights (KVKK art. 11)

You have the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, request information about it, learn the purpose of processing, know the third parties to whom it is transferred at home or abroad, request correction of incomplete or inaccurate data, request erasure, request that these actions be notified to third parties, object to results arising from automated analysis, and claim compensation for damages.

Send requests to selwareapps@gmail.com; they are concluded within 30 days at the latest.
