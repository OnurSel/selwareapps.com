About the app

Pip & Pop is a collection of mini games for two people on one phone. The bottom half of the screen is yours, the top half belongs to your opponent. There are 40 games, from air hockey to penalty shootouts, sumo to tank duels, and none of them is locked behind a payment.

If you're on your own, every game can be played against a bot on easy, medium or hard. No internet needed, no account, no in-app purchases. No ads while you're playing.

What's inside

  • 40 mini games, all unlocked
  • Tournament mode
  • Challenge mode: 100 levels
  • Easy, medium and hard bots
  • A wardrobe of hats and accessories
  • Plays offline
