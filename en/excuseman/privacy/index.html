Privacy policy

ExcuseMan: Not My Fault

Effective date: October 20, 2026

In short

ExcuseMan is a game developed by Selware. As the developer, we do not collect any personal data from you. The game has no server of its own, does not ask you to create an account, and contains no analytics, measurement, or crash-reporting tools. The game can be played offline.

The game is free and shows ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, and Google collects some information from your device in order to show them (see below). Without an internet connection no ads load, and the game is still fully playable.

When do ads appear?

  • Never during play. There are no ads in the middle of a level, when you lose a heart, or during dialogue. There are no banner ads.
  • Interstitial ads can appear only on the level-complete screen and on the end screen of Overtime mode. They never appear in the first 10 minutes after you open the game; after that, at most once every three levels (in Overtime, at most once every two runs).
  • Rewarded ads are entirely optional and appear only in these offers: "continue from the last checkpoint with full hearts", "double this level's earnings", and "+1 life" in Overtime. You never have to watch an ad to progress.

The following stays only in your phone's own storage, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you delete the game:

Leaderboard and achievements (Google Play Games / Game Center)

The game can work with Google Play Games on Android and Game Center on iPhone. If you are signed in to these services, the game sends the achievements you earn and your Overtime survival time to them, and you appear on the leaderboard with your player name. Google or Apple processes this information under your own account and their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store it. If you do not want to use these services, you can play the full game without signing in.

Permissions

The game does not ask for camera, location, contacts, microphone, or file access. On Android, the ad library adds the internet and advertising ID permissions, used to load ads. On iPhone, your advertising identifier is used only if you allow it in the iOS permission prompt; if you decline, ads are still shown but are not personalized.

European Union and United Kingdom

In these regions the game shows Google's consent form on first launch, and you choose whether to consent to personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy Options.

Ads and third parties

Google AdMob (Google LLC) serves the ads. To show ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance, it may collect your advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / iOS IDFA, if you allow it), IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions from your device. Google processes this data under its own policies:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings. Ads shown are limited to content suitable for general and teen audiences.

The game contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or social-network libraries. Apart from the ad data that goes to Google, and the scores and achievements that go to Play Games / Game Center if you are signed in, none of your data reaches us or anyone else.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The game itself collects no personal data from any user, including children; the ad data that goes to Google is described above.

Deleting your data

All of the game's data on your device is deleted when you delete the game. You can also erase your progress with Settings → Reset progress. We hold no data about you to delete. For data in your Google or Apple accounts, use those companies' own tools.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this address and the effective date will be updated.
