About the app

Prayer times are calculated on your phone, so they stay correct even without an internet connection. If you'd rather not share your location, pick your city from the list.

Read the Quran as Mushaf pages or with a translation, listen verse by verse from 15 reciters and practise with the Memorization tool. The home screen shows the dua, hadith and names of the day as stories, plus a moon phase card.

There are also prayer, missed-prayer and fasting trackers, a zakat calculator, a For Women section, a Hijri calendar, nearby mosques, duas, the 99 Names and a dhikr counter.
