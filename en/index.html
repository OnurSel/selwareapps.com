A bit about me

Hi, I'm Onur. I study computer engineering at Abdullah Gül University in Kayseri, Türkiye, and this is my final year. Between classes and my own projects, it's shaping up to be a busy one.

I didn't start making apps with some big plan. There were small things in daily life that kept bugging me; I thought "there has to be an easier way" and tried building that way myself. Over time, that curiosity turned into the apps you see here. Hearing that something I made helped a complete stranger is still my favourite part of it.

But my life isn't all screens. I really love nature, and whenever I get the chance I head outdoors. Getting away from the noise of the city and spending some time in nature is the best way I know to clear my head.

I love travelling just as much. Seeing a new place, wandering its streets, getting to know its people and its food gives me something new every time. And on evenings at home, I like to unwind with a good series or film.
