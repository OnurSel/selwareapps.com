Privacy policy

Ezan: Prayer Times

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Information we collect and use

Location information

With your permission, your device's approximate location is used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction.

Location information is processed only on your device. It is not sent to the developer's servers and is not stored by the developer.

You may use the city selection feature instead of granting location permission.

Nearby mosques: Only when you open the "Nearby Mosques" screen, your approximate location (coordinates rounded to about 1 km) is sent to free OpenStreetMap-based search services (Photon by komoot, with Overpass as a fallback) to list mosques around you. This information does not reach the developer's servers and is not stored by the developer. The "Show on map" and "Directions" buttons open the map app on your phone, whose own privacy policy applies.

Health and worship records

Period records in the "For Women" section, prayer, fasting and missed-prayer tracking, memorization progress and the values you enter in the zakat calculator are stored only on your device. They are never sent to any server and the developer cannot access them.

Application preferences

Preferences such as language, theme, calculation method, selected city and notification settings are stored only on your device. These preferences are used to provide the application's features and preserve your user experience.

Advertising and third-party services

The application may display banner advertisements through Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers and application interaction data to provide advertisements, personalize them to your interests, measure advertising performance, prevent fraud and improve its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your consent is requested through Google's consent form before ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Policy → Ad privacy options button in the app. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow it; you can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. On Android you can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Internet usage

Core features such as prayer time calculation may work without an internet connection. Some features, including Quran audio, Quran Radio, nearby mosques, the app update check and advertisements, require an internet connection. Quran audio is streamed from Islamic Network (cdn.islamic.network), which may process technical connection data (such as your IP address) under its own terms.

Data sharing

The application developer does not directly collect, sell or share your personal data. Third-party service providers such as Google AdMob may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms of service.

Data storage

Application preferences and settings are stored locally on your device. Locally stored information may be removed when you uninstall the application.

Children's privacy

The application is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child's personal information has been processed improperly may contact us using the email address below.

Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy may be updated when the application's features or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page, and the last updated date displayed at the top of the page will be revised.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: selwareapps@gmail.com
