About the app

Finansel keeps your income and expenses in one place. If you don't feel like typing, take a photo of the receipt or say “groceries 250”, and the amount, date and category fill themselves in.

You can use it without an account, in which case your records stay only on your phone. With an account they are backed up, and you can keep a shared budget with your family.

What's inside

  • Receipt scanning from camera or gallery
  • Add records by voice
  • Budgets by category
  • Daily, monthly and yearly statistics
  • Shared family budget
  • Works without an account
