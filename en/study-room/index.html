About the app

Study Room is the simplest version of studying together: open a room, send the code or link to your friends, and everyone sees the same timer. When someone starts it, it starts for everybody, and the break comes for everyone at once.

Whoever created the room can set the focus and break lengths, and can let the next phase start by itself. There is also a shared task list and a view of who is in the room. You don't need an account, you just type a name and go.

Your streak and today's focus time are kept on this device only, because without accounts the server has no record of who you are. Rooms close after 24 hours.

What's inside

  • A shared timer that runs in step for everyone
  • The room's creator sets focus and break lengths
  • Optional automatic start of the next phase
  • A shared task list
  • A live list of who is in the room
  • Streak and daily focus time on this device
  • No account, nothing to install
