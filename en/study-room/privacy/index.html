Privacy policy

Study Room

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Developer: Onur Sel (Selware) · Contact: selwareapps@gmail.com

Short version

Study Room has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. You type a display name, create or join a room, and that is all it knows about you.

1. What the server holds

  • The display name you type and a random anonymous ID created for you, so the room can show who is in it.
  • The room's shared content: the room code, the timer state and the shared task list.
  • This data is kept in a short-lived store and is tied to the room, which closes 24 hours after it was created.
  • We do not ask for your email, phone, location, contacts or any real identity. Please do not put personal information in names or tasks, because everyone in the room can read them.

2. What stays on your device

Your streak, today's focus time, the list of rooms you visited, your sound/notification choices and your anonymous ID are kept in your browser's local storage. They are never uploaded. Clearing your browser data deletes them (and you will then no longer be recognised as the owner of rooms you created).

3. Cookies, tracking and ads

The app sets no cookies, includes no ad network and no third-party analytics or tracking.

4. Hosting

The service runs on Render (application and cache), with the database on Neon. Like any web host, they may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses for security and operation. We do not use these logs to identify you.

5. Notifications

If you turn on desktop notifications, they are generated by your own browser from the timer in the page. Nothing is sent to us.

6. Children

Study Room is not directed at children and collects no age information.

7. Contact

Questions or requests: selwareapps@gmail.com
